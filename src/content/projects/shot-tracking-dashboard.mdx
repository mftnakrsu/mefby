## Overview

A production dashboard I built at Hagia for a series in production. It shows where every episode, scene and shot stands, so producers and supervisors can follow the progress without collecting spreadsheets by hand.

## What it shows

Progress for each episode, scene and shot; a matrix of scene progress across the whole series; the workload of each owner and artist; and progress figures by day and by week. The numbers follow the team's own tracking files and update as those files change.

## How it is built

A FastAPI backend reads the production's tracking files from Google Drive through the Drive API and writes the generated reports, and a Next.js front end draws the dashboard on top of it. The team keeps working in the files it already uses, and the dashboard needs nothing extra from them.
