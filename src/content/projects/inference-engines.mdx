## Overview

A course in Turkish that takes an engineer from first principles to expert level in serving large language models with vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp and Ollama. It has 25 chapters and a Turkish–English glossary, about 138,000 words in all. Each chapter rests on research checked against current sources, and a separate agent reviewed it adversarially before it was accepted.

## What it covers

How prefill and decode differ and what the KV cache costs; quantization formats from GGUF K-quants to AWQ, GPTQ and FP8; PagedAttention, continuous batching, RadixAttention and speculative decoding; and parallelism across GPUs. A chapter on benchmark method defines time to first token, time per output token, inter-token latency and goodput, so that engines can be compared fairly.

## The labs

The course has two reference machines: a workstation GPU with 8 GB, where the labs run Qwen3-1.7B, and an NVIDIA H200 with 141 GB, sized for 70B models in FP8 and for mixture-of-experts models. Lab scripts drive the load, watch the GPU and turn the measurements into a report.
